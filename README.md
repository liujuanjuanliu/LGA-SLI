# LGA-SLI
LGA-SLI: local-global spatiotemporal aggregation and short-long temporal interaction for facial expression recognition
Dynamic evolution of facial expressions in natural scenes is often concealed within short-term segments, while the complete emotional semantics depend on the ordered coherence across multiple segments. To address the challenges of short-term segment concealment and long-term sequence dependency in dynamic facial expression recognition (FER), a FER network based on local-global spatiotemporal aggregation and short-long temporal interaction (LGA-SLI) is proposed. LGA-SLI is mainly composed of intra-segment local-global semantic spatiotemporal aggregation structure (IA-LGA) and inter-segment short-long term temporal interaction structure (IE-SLI). In IA-LGA, hybrid pooling operation is employed to extract local salient features of frame-level spatial sub-blocks. Additionally, a global spatiotemporal semantic guidance strategy based on a two-stage Graph Convolutional Network (GCN) is designed to capture hierarchical spatiotemporal aggregated semantics from local to global. In IE-SLI, cross-attention mechanism is embedded to bidirectionally enhance short-term temporal semantics between adjacent segments. Furthermore, a long-term spatiotemporal semantic guidance strategy based on GCN is developed to enable hierarchical temporal modeling of short-long term spatiotemporal semantics. Experimental results on the real scene expression datasets AFEW and DFEW show that LGA-SLI achieves (weighted average recall) UAR/ (weighted average recall) WAR of 53.73%/58.82% on AFEW and 60.21%/70.42% on DFEW, which are 11.58%/13.09%, 13.93% /13.94% higher than the baseline network. Compared with related methods, LGA-SLI effectively improves the performance of dynamic FER by capturing hierarchical emotional association semantics and leveraging dedicated intra-segment and inter-segment guidance strategies.
